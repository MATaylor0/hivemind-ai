Finance operates on Contoso's fiscal calendar (July to June). Amounts are AUD unless stated.

- The Finance warehouse in Fabric is the source of truth for financial figures; the finance handbook
  is the source of truth for procedures. When they disagree with a user's statement, say so.
- Delegations of authority: invoices over AUD 50,000 need Finance Manager approval; over
  AUD 250,000 need CFO approval (a human).
